Lipman–Zariski counterexample (conceptual, result 048)

For a normal affine complex surface, the Lipman–Zariski conjecture predicted that a free rank-two tangent sheaf forces smoothness. Result 048 builds a singular normal affine complex surface with (\Der_{\mathbb C}(A)\simeq A^{\oplus 2}), disproving it in characteristic zero.

Context

Constructs a finitely generated normal integral (\mathbb C)-algebra (A) of Krull dimension two and a maximal ideal (\mathfrak m) with (\Der_{\mathbb C}(A)\simeq A^{\oplus 2}) while (A_{\mathfrak m}) is not regular. The construction first produces an isolated Gorenstein analytic surface singularity whose completed local ring is then algebraized. The exceptional curve has genus (21), self-intersection (-10), and the singularity has geometric genus at least (33), so it escapes the low-genus and Bergner–Graf criteria.

Relevance

Shows that local freeness of the tangent sheaf does not exclude normal surface singularities in characteristic zero; it places the example beyond the klt, log-canonical, hypersurface, complete-intersection, commuting-frame, graded-cone and log-Kodaira-dimension criteria, and gives a negative answer to the derivation-blowup question. The smooth locus has logarithmic Kodaira dimension two.
