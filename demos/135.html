Sharp homogeneous depth-five complexity of matrix products (conceptual, result 135)

Over every characteristic-zero field, the $(1,1)$ entry of a product of $n$ independent $n\times n$ variable matrices requires $n^{\Theta(\sqrt n)}$ gates in homogeneous depth-five sum--product circuits.

Context

Over every characteristic-zero field, the $(1,1)$ entry of a product of $n$ independent $n\times n$ variable matrices requires $n^{\Theta(\sqrt n)}$ gates in homogeneous depth-five sum--product circuits. The bound is sharp: it allows shared gates and bottom linear forms involving all variables, so no additional saving comes from those features in this depth. It places iterated matrix multiplication firmly in the superpolynomial regime for bounded-depth homogeneous arithmetic circuits.

Relevance

Gives a sharp superpolynomial lower bound for a natural polynomial family in homogeneous depth-five circuits, clarifying the power and limits of bounded-depth arithmetic computation, and providing a benchmark for algebraic complexity and circuit lower-bound techniques.
