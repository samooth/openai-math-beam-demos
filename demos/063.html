The generalized Mukai conjecture (conceptual, result 063)

For a smooth complex projective Fano manifold of dimension (n), Picard number (\rho) and pseudoindex (\iota) (least anticanonical degree of a rational curve), the conjecture requires (\rho(\iota-1)\le n), with equality exactly for ((\mathbb P^{\iota-1})^\rho). Tune (n,\rho,\iota) to test the inequality and see the allowed region.

Context

Proves the generalized Mukai conjecture: every positive-dimensional smooth complex projective Fano manifold of dimension (n), Picard number (\rho) and pseudoindex (\iota) satisfies (\rho(\iota-1)\le n), with equality exactly for ((\mathbb P^{\iota-1})^\rho). Here the pseudoindex is the least anticanonical degree of a rational curve.

Relevance

Bounds the Picard number of a Fano manifold in terms of its dimension and pseudoindex, and characterizes the extremal case as a product of projective spaces. This is a central structural result in Fano geometry, guiding classification and the study of rational curves, and it interacts with boundedness and rigidity questions for Fano varieties.
