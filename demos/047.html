Zariski cancellation and affine fibrations over (\mathbb C) (conceptual, result 047)

Cancellation asks whether (X\times \mathbb A^1\cong \mathbb A^{n+1}) forces (X\cong \mathbb A^n). Result 047 gives a fourfold counterexample and nontrivial affine three-space fibrations over (\mathbb A^1) and (\mathbb A^2).

Context

Constructs an integral complex affine fourfold (X\not\cong\mathbb A^4) with (X\times\mathbb A^1\cong\mathbb A^5), disproving affine-space cancellation over (\mathbb C) in dimension four. It also disproves the Dolgachev–Weisfeiler affine-fibration conjecture: smooth surjections (X\to\mathbb A^1) and (\mathbb A^5\to\mathbb A^2) have every residue-field fiber isomorphic to affine three-space but are not Zariski-locally trivial.

Relevance

Shows that affine space is not cancellable in dimension four and that fiberwise (\mathbb A^3)-fibrations need not be Zariski-locally trivial. This affects classification of affine varieties, exotic affine spaces, and questions about algebraic vector bundles and triviality of fibrations.
