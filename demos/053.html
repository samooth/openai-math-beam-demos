Pixton completeness counterexample (conceptual, result 053)

On (\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}), Pixton's span proposes all tautological relations. We construct a formal strata class (Y) that vanishes in rational Chow (hence cohomology) but is not in that span. Context

Disproves the Chow and rational-cohomological forms of Pixton's original completeness conjecture. For (g=10^{60}), (D_*=\binom g3), (n=3D_*), an explicitly defined class (Y\in\mathrm{Salg}_{g,n}^{2D_*}) satisfies (q_{g,n}(Y)=0) in rational Chow (and hence (H^{4D_*})), while (Y\notin\Pspan_{g,n}). The proof uses a formal exterior coefficient ring with independent odd cubic coefficients (\theta_{ijk}) to detect nonmembership, and a surface Quot-scheme nilpotence identity to prove vanishing.

Relevance

Shows that Pixton's graph-formula span is not complete: there are genuine tautological relations outside it. It separates completeness (all relations among generators) from the Gorenstein property, and motivates refined relation systems or larger spans that include the new cubic/alternating diagonal corrections. The counterexample lives on the full stable compactification, not only compact type.
