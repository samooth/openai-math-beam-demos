Subpolynomial dimension reduction in (L_p) (conceptual, result 094)

For every fixed (1<p<\infty) and distortion (D>1), every (n)-point subset of real (L_p) embeds into (\ell_p^d) with distortion at most (D) and dimension (d=n^{o(1)}), answering Naor's sublinear-dimension question for (p\ne2). Exact embeddings can require worst-case dimension (\Theta(n^2)) when (p\ne2).

Context

For (p>1), (p\ne2), the least dimension needed to embed every (n)-point subset of real (L_p) with distortion (D) is bounded as follows. For each fixed (D>1), there is (C=C(p,D)) such that the dimension lies between (\log n/\log(1+2D)) and (\exp(C(\log n)^{\gamma(p)})) for (n\ge2), with (\gamma(p)=2-p) for (p<2) and (1-2/p) for (p>2). At distortion (1) and (n\ge9), it lies between (\lfloor (n-1)/4\rfloor^2) and (\binom n2). The least dimension is attained, and (\log d/\log n\to0) for (D>1) while (\to2) for (D=1).

Relevance

Dimension reduction in (L_p) governs approximate nearest-neighbor search, compressed sensing, and metric embedding algorithms. The (D>1) bound shows approximate (\ell_p) geometry is compressible to subpolynomial dimension, while (D=1) retains a quadratic worst case, exposing a sharp phase transition at exactness. The exponent (\gamma(p)) captures how the (p)-dependent geometry controls the subpolynomial rate.
