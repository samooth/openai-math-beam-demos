Bounded-distortion L1 embeddings of planar and bounded-treewidth graphs (conceptual, result 089)

Resolves the planar and bounded-treewidth cases of the Gupta--Newman--Rabinovich--Sinclair conjecture: shortest-path metrics of finite connected graphs embed into real (L_1) with universal distortion for planar graphs and distortion depending only on treewidth for bounded-treewidth graphs. The corresponding multicommodity flow--cut gaps are uniformly bounded.

Context

Resolves the planar and bounded-treewidth cases of the Gupta--Newman--Rabinovich--Sinclair conjecture. Shortest-path metrics of finite connected graphs with arbitrary positive edge lengths embed into real (L_1) with universal distortion for planar graphs, and distortion depending only on treewidth for bounded-treewidth graphs. The corresponding multicommodity flow--cut gaps are uniformly bounded.

Relevance

Metric embeddings into (L_1) govern multicommodity flow--cut gaps, sparsest-cut approximations, and algorithm design on graph classes. Constant (planar) and treewidth-dependent (bounded treewidth) distortion bounds replace exponential worst-case gaps with uniform guarantees, enabling efficient approximation and exact algorithms on these graph classes.
