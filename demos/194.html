Lech's multiplicity conjecture (conceptual, result 194)

For a flat local homomorphism (R\to S) of nonzero Noetherian local rings, Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity cannot decrease: (e(R)\le e(S)). The demo models the multiplicative factor (e(S)=e(R)\cdot e(S/\mathfrak mS)) and checks the inequality.

Context

Proves (e(R)\le e(S)) for every flat local homomorphism of nonzero Noetherian local rings, where (e) is Hilbert--Samuel multiplicity. This resolves Lech's conjecture in every dimension and characteristic.

Relevance

Shows multiplicity is monotone under flat local maps: the closed fiber contributes a multiplicity factor at least one, so the source multiplicity is a lower bound for the target. This gives a dimension- and characteristic-independent comparison tool in commutative algebra and algebraic geometry.
