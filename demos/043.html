P=W for fixed-determinant Higgs moduli (conceptual, result 043)

For a smooth projective complex curve (C) of genus (g\ge2), the non-abelian Hodge correspondence identifies Higgs moduli and character varieties. The perverse filtration (P_k) on Higgs cohomology matches the weight filtration (W) on the character variety: (P_k = W_{2k}=W_{2k+1}). Tune rank, genus, degree and indexing to compare the two filtrations.

Context

Proves (P_k=W_{2k}=W_{2k+1}) on the full rational cohomology of smooth coprime fixed-determinant, trace-free Higgs moduli spaces and their character varieties for composite ranks over smooth projective complex curves of genus at least two. This includes variant cohomology and, together with the known prime-rank theorems, establishes the fixed-determinant (P=W) conjecture in every coprime rank.

Relevance

Identifies the perverse filtration on Higgs-moduli cohomology with the weight filtration on the character variety via non-abelian Hodge, so topological invariants of the two moduli spaces agree under index doubling; extends the fixed-determinant (P=W) conjecture to all coprime ranks, including composite ranks and variant cohomology.
