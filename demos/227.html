Critical SK autocorrelation processes and dynamics across the temperature transition (conceptual, result 227)

Zero-field Gaussian Sherrington–Kirkpatrick heat-bath dynamics: high-temperature spectral gaps and cutoff, critical slowing down with universal autocorrelation limits, and stretched-exponential barriers below the transition.

Context

For zero-field Gaussian SK heat-bath dynamics with rate-one updates per spin, at fixed $0\le\beta<1$ the worst-start mixing time is controlled on the $\log n$ scale, with mixing time $n^{2/3+o(1)}$ at $\beta=1$ and stretched-exponential mixing from Gibbs-sampled starts for $\beta>1$, in probability over disorder. At criticality, rescaled stationary and quench autocorrelation processes have universal random limits for Gaussian and Rademacher disorder, and the quench limit relaxes to the stationary limit. The formalization also gives dimension-independent Poincaré inequalities for $0<\beta<1$ and mixing obstructions at $\beta>1$.

Relevance

Captures the full temperature transition of spin-glass dynamics: fast high-temperature relaxation and cutoff, critical slowing down with universal autocorrelation processes, and exponentially long barriers in the glass phase.
