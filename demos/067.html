Campana–Peternell in dimension six (conceptual, result 067)

A smooth Fano variety with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous; in dimension six this is now proved. Tune dimension, Fano property, nefness of (T_X), and a candidate homogeneous type to see when the conjecture forces (X\simeq G/P).

Context

Proves the Campana--Peternell conjecture in complex dimension six: every smooth connected complex projective Fano sixfold with nef tangent bundle is rational homogeneous. Equivalently, if (X) is Fano and (T_X) is nef, then (X\simeq G/P) for a semisimple algebraic group (G) and a parabolic subgroup (P). The proof combines Mori theory, varieties of minimal rational tangents (VMRT), and the geometry of nef tangent bundles; dimension six is the newly settled case, extending the previously known low-dimensional cases.

Relevance

Classifies Fano manifolds with nef tangent bundle through dimension six: their rational curves and tangent directions are forced to be homogeneous, determining automorphism groups, moduli, and Picard/anticanonical data. It separates genuine rational homogeneous spaces (projective spaces, quadrics, Grassmannians, and exceptional (G/P)) from non-homogeneous Fano varieties, for which the tangent bundle must have a negative direction. The result supports the full Campana--Peternell conjecture in arbitrary dimension via VMRT.
