Quasi-Riemann hypothesis: zero-free region Re s > 7/8

Every Dirichlet L-function, including ζ(s), is zero-free in the half-plane Re s > 7/8. Drag the vertical line or use the slider to set the real-part threshold; the shaded region is the zero-free half-plane.

Context

A uniform zero-free region for all Dirichlet L-functions. The same half-plane holds for finite-order Hecke L-functions over Q(√−3); a companion gives an alternate proof for Re s > 11/12. Landau–Siegel zeros are uniformly excluded.

Relevance

Zero-free regions control the error term in the prime number theorem, nonvanishing of L-values, and class-number bounds. A uniform 7/8 threshold gives strong unconditional control of prime distribution and gaps.
