Modularity over imaginary quadratic fields (result 030)

Elliptic curve (E: y^2 = x^3 + a x + b) over an imaginary quadratic field (K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt D)). The demo computes local Hecke parameters (a_{\mathfrak p}=q+1-#E(\mathbb F_q)) for unramified primes; modularity says these are the eigenvalues of a Bianchi modular form.

Context

Proves the modularity conjecture for elliptic curves over imaginary quadratic fields: every elliptic curve over every imaginary quadratic field is modular, with matching local parameters at every place.

Relevance

Equates (L(E/K,s)) with the L-function of an automorphic representation of (\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb A_K)); local factors, including bad and archimedean places, agree. This links arithmetic of elliptic curves over imaginary quadratic fields to Bianchi modular forms and the Langlands program.
