Finite symmetric tensor categories and the Verlinde tower

Conceptual interactive view of the tower ( \mathrm{Ver}_{p}(k)\subset \mathrm{Ver}_{p^2}(k)\subset \cdots ) and a fiber functor from a finite symmetric tensor category (\mathcal C) to a finite level (\mathrm{Ver}_{p^n}(k)). The level may depend on (\mathcal C).

Context

Proves that every finite symmetric tensor category over an algebraically closed field \(k\) of characteristic \(p>0) admits a (k)-linear exact faithful strong symmetric monoidal fiber functor to a higher Verlinde category (\mathrm{Ver}_{p^n}(k)). The level may depend on the category, and the theorem includes characteristic two, resolving the finite case of the Benson–Etingof–Ostrik conjecture.

Relevance

Classifies finite incompressible symmetric tensor categories as (possibly plus) higher Verlinde categories, and in characteristic two gives uniqueness of the fiber functor into the union of the tower.
