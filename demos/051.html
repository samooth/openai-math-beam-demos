Kobayashi’s canonical-ampleness conjecture (conceptual, result 051)

For a compact connected Kähler manifold (X), the absence of nonconstant entire curves (Brody hyperbolicity) forces the canonical bundle (K_X) to be ample and (X) to be projective. Explore presets and tune dimension/genus to see when hyperbolicity and canonical ampleness hold.

Context

Proves Kobayashi's canonical-ampleness conjecture: every compact connected Kähler manifold of positive complex dimension with no nonconstant entire curve has ample canonical bundle and is therefore projective. This resolves positively Kobayashi's canonical-ampleness conjecture in the smooth compact Kähler category.

Relevance

Connects a restriction on one-dimensional holomorphic maps (Brody hyperbolicity) to positivity of top-degree forms and projective algebraicity. It implies pluricanonical bundles (K_X^{\otimes m}) are globally generated for (m\ge n+2), and supports finite-cover product decompositions when the universal cover has a semialgebraic realization.
