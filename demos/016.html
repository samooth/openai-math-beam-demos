Zilber–Pink: atypical subvarieties (conceptual)

A Hodge-generic curve meets special loci (CM points, torsion cosets). Intersections with excess dimension are atypical; Zilber–Pink predicts only finitely many maximal atypical subvarieties. Drag controls to see the finite highlighted set.

Context

Proves the abelian Zilber–Pink conjecture over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$: every irreducible subvariety of an abelian variety has finitely many maximal atypical subvarieties relative to its smallest containing torsion coset. Also proves the full curve case in the Siegel threefold $\mathcal A_2$ for Hodge-generic curves over $\overline{\mathbb Q}$, without boundary or reduction assumptions.

Relevance

Gives finiteness of atypical intersections such as CM points and elliptic-square loci on curves in moduli spaces; supports arithmetic and unlikely-intersection problems.
