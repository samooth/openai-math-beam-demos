Anticanonical nonvanishing under smooth semipositivity (conceptual, result 068)

If \(X\) is a smooth connected complex projective variety and \(-K_X\) admits a smooth Hermitian metric with nonnegative curvature, then \(H^0(X,-mK_X)\ne0\) for some \(m>0). Tune the curvature of a Hermitian metric on (-K_X) to see when smooth semipositivity forces a nonzero anticanonical section.

Context

Proves anticanonical nonvanishing from smooth semipositivity in every dimension: if \(X\) is a smooth connected complex projective variety and \(-K_X\) admits a smooth Hermitian metric with nonnegative curvature, then \(H^0(X,-mK_X)\ne0\) for some \(m>0). The proof converts invariant cohomology into twisted differential forms and descends them before conversion; a companion removes a fixed pseudoeffective error from an unbounded sequence of effective twists. Thus smooth semipositivity forces a nonzero section of a positive tensor power of the anticanonical bundle in every dimension.

Relevance

Turns a differential hypothesis on (-K_X) into an algebraic conclusion: a variety whose anticanonical bundle carries a smooth Hermitian metric of nonnegative curvature cannot have all anticanonical powers vanish. This constrains the geometry and moduli of semipositive varieties, supports abundance-type statements in the semipositive regime, and separates smooth (Hermitian) positivity from mere algebraic (nef) positivity by producing a concrete nonzero section.
