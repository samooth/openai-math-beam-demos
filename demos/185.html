Counterexamples to infinite matroid intersection and packing/covering (conceptual, result 185)

In ZFC there are two self-dual partitional matroids on a countably infinite ground set whose independent sets cannot cover the whole set. Equivalently, the pair does not satisfy infinite matroid intersection, and it admits no packing/covering partition.

Context

Disproves the unrestricted infinite matroid intersection and packing/covering conjectures in ZFC, using two self-dual partitional matroids on a countably infinite ground set. The same examples answer Joó’s partitional-matroid question negatively. They are neither finitary nor cofinitary, so Nash-Williams’ original finitary conjecture remains outside the result.

Relevance

Gives a negative ZFC answer to Joó’s question for two partitional matroids on a common countable ground set, and separates the unrestricted infinite setting from the finitary case where intersection theorems still hold.

Conceptual model: a putative independent covering forces, at each copy of the local matroid $Q$, a large side of the next bundle with strictly smaller ordinal rank. Repeating gives an infinite strictly decreasing ordinal sequence, which is impossible. The finite counter is a truncated analogue of that descent.
