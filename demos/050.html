Griffiths positivity counterexample (conceptual, result 050)

On \(Q=\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\), an ample rank-two bundle \(E_m\) is built for every \(m>0) (a power-pullback twist of a base bundle). For all sufficiently large (m), no smooth Hermitian metric on (E_m) has strictly Griffiths-positive curvature. Tune (m) and the illustrative threshold to see ampleness persist while positivity eventually fails.

Context

Disproves Griffiths' positivity conjecture on the quadric surface: constructs ample rank-two bundles \(E_m\) on \(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\) with no smooth Hermitian metric of strictly Griffiths-positive curvature. For each \(m>0\), \(E_m\) is ample (indeed \(2(3m+1)\)-very ample via a closed embedding), while for all large \(m\) the curvature of any Hermitian metric fails the positivity condition \(\langle i\Theta(E,h)v,v\rangle(\xi,\bar\xi)>0) for some tangent vector (\xi) and fiber vector (v). The obstruction comes from the plurisubharmonic trace and noncollapse of an extracted global dual form.

Relevance

Separates algebraic ampleness from differential positivity: a bundle can be ample (with positivity of (\mathcal O(1)) on (\mathbb P(E))) yet admit no Hermitian metric whose curvature is strictly Griffiths positive. This refutes the natural metric strengthening of the ampleness--positivity correspondence, constrains curvature-based approaches to vector-bundle positivity (Griffiths/Nakano), and highlights that positivity of curvature is a strictly stronger condition than ampleness in rank (\ge 2).
