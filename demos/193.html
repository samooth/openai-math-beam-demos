Serre's intersection-multiplicity conjecture (conceptual, result 193)

For a regular local ring (R) of dimension (d) and nonzero modules (M,N) with (\length_R(M\otimes_R N)<\infty\) and \(\dim M+\dim N=d\), Serre's multiplicity is \(\chi^R(M,N)=\sum_{i=0}^d (-1)^i\length_R\Tor_i^R(M,N)\). The demo evaluates this Euler characteristic on small explicit examples.

Context

Result 193 proves \(\chi^R(M,N)>0) for every commutative Noetherian regular local ring, including ramified mixed characteristic, without liftability or smoothness hypotheses. The higher Tor terms enter with alternating signs, so positivity is not automatic from the degree-zero term.
