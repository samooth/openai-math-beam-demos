The three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture (conceptual, result 078)

The Bochner–Riesz multipliers of order \(\delta\) are bounded on \(L^p(\mathbb R^3)\) in the strict range \(\delta>\max\{3|1/p-1/2|-1/2,0\}\). The critical case is \(L^3\): boundedness holds for every \(\delta>0). Explore the admissible region and the radial multiplier profile.

Context

Resolves the three-dimensional Bochner–Riesz conjecture in its strict-order formulation: the Bochner–Riesz multipliers of order \(\delta\) are bounded on \(L^p(\mathbb R^3)\) for every \(1\le p\le\infty\) whenever \(\delta>\max\{3|1/p-1/2|-1/2,0\}). The main new ingredient is boundedness on (L^3(\mathbb R^3)) for every positive order; interpolation and duality then give the full conjectured strict range.

Relevance

Settles a central problem in harmonic analysis: the optimal (L^p) boundedness of radial Fourier multipliers supported on the unit ball in (\mathbb R^3). It fixes the threshold between bounded and unbounded behavior, confirms the critical (L^3) estimate with arbitrarily small positive loss, and feeds into restriction theory, local smoothing, and dispersive estimates for the wave equation.
