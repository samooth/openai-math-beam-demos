Donovan's conjecture over fields and complete mixed-characteristic DVRs (conceptual, result 203)

Donovan's conjecture: for a fixed algebraically closed field $K$ of characteristic $p$ and a bound $M$ on defect-group order, there are only finitely many $K$-linear Morita equivalence classes of blocks of finite groups. The integral form holds over complete mixed-characteristic DVRs.

Context

Proves Donovan's conjecture over every algebraically closed field $K$ of characteristic $p>0$: for fixed $K$ and a bound on defect-group order, blocks of finite groups represent only finitely many $K$-linear Morita equivalence classes, including non-abelian defect groups and $p=2$. Also proves integral finiteness over $W(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$ and over each fixed complete discrete valuation ring of characteristic zero with algebraically closed residue field of characteristic $p$, including ramified rings.

Relevance

Gives a uniform finiteness statement for blocks under bounded defect, supporting classification of blocks, derived and Morita equivalences, and the broader modular representation theory of finite groups. The mixed-characteristic integral form connects field-theoretic and lift-theoretic invariants.
