Integral density on curve character varieties (result 027)

Conceptual view of Litt's integral-density question for SL_r character varieties of curves: integral points accumulate in every component as the height bound grows, including prescribed quasi-unipotent boundary classes.

Context

Resolves the determinant-one curve case of Litt's integral-density question. For every smooth connected complex algebraic curve and every rank, integral points become Zariski dense in every component of its SL_r character variety over the full ring of integers of one number field. Prescribed quasi-unipotent boundary conjugacy classes are allowed, including nonsemisimple classes.

Relevance

Settles the determinant-one curve case of Litt's question over one number field, including prescribed quasi-unipotent (possibly nonsemisimple) boundary classes, with integral points Zariski dense in every component.
