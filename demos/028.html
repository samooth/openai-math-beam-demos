The Gaussian moat conjecture

Gaussian primes in the complex plane; edges connect primes at distance at most D. Each color is a connected component of this bounded-step graph.

Context

Proves the Gaussian moat conjecture: no infinite walk through distinct Gaussian primes can have uniformly bounded steps. More strongly, for every distance bound D, the graph joining Gaussian primes at distance at most D has uniformly bounded finite component sizes, depending only on D, including primes on the coordinate axes.

Relevance

Gives a uniform finite-component bound for bounded-step walks on Gaussian primes, a quantitative form of the moat conjecture with consequences for prime distribution in the Gaussian integers.
