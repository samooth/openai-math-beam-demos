Approximate counting of common integer polymatroid bases (conceptual, result 114)

A fully polynomial randomized approximation scheme counts common integer bases of two integral polymatroids of equal total rank, using exact rank-value oracles. Binary capacities are handled directly. This demo illustrates the matroid special case: common spanning trees of two graphs on the same vertex and edge sets.

Context

For $E=\{1,\dots,n\}$, two normalized nondecreasing submodular rank functions $r_1,r_2:2^E\to\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$ with common total rank $R=r_1(E)=r_2(E)$ define the common bases $\Omega=\{x\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^E:x(E)=R,;x(A)\le r_i(A) \forall A\subseteq E, i=1,2\}$. The FPRAS outputs $\hat Z$ with $(1-\varepsilon)Z\le \hat Z\le(1+\varepsilon)Z$ with probability at least $1-\delta$, and outputs $0$ whenever $Z=0$. Costs are polynomial in $n$, the binary encoding length, $\varepsilon^{-1}$, and $\log(\delta^{-1})$. Capacities are encoded in binary and are not expanded into labelled copies. In the matroid case, bases are sets of rank size; here they are common spanning trees of two graphs sharing one edge set.

Relevance

This unifies matroid intersection with capacitated allocation problems and gives the first polynomial-time randomized approximation for counting common bases under binary-encoded submodular constraints. It avoids the exponential blowup of expanding capacities into labelled elements and preserves each integer vector with weight one. The companion results cover common matroid bases and cell-bounded contingency tables; the present reduction integrates out all but polynomially many discrete variables and verifies the needed quadratic inequalities.
