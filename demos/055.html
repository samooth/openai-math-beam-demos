Toda's Gepner conjecture and large-volume stability (conceptual, result 055)

For a smooth complex quintic threefold, Toda's Gepner conjecture asks for a numerical Bridgeland stability condition with phase shift (2/5). More generally, on smooth projective threefolds with trivial canonical bundle, numerical stability conditions exist at sufficiently large volume with central charges built from the ordinary or square-root Todd class. Tune the parameters below to see when the Gepner phase and large-volume stability hold.

Context

Proves Toda's Gepner conjecture for every smooth complex quintic threefold, constructing a numerical Bridgeland stability condition with the prescribed phase shift (2/5). Also constructs numerical Bridgeland stability conditions at every sufficiently large volume on all smooth projective complex threefolds with trivial canonical bundle, with the exact ordinary and square-root-Todd central charges.

Relevance

Supplies the stability conditions needed to define Donaldson--Thomas invariants and Gepner-point moduli on Calabi--Yau threefolds. The phase shift (2/5) matches the quintic's Gepner point, while the large-volume construction gives a concrete chamber for wall-crossing and enumerative invariants on CY threefolds with trivial canonical bundle.
