Connects height fluctuations of the balanced six-vertex model to a universal log-correlated Gaussian free field, with an explicit variance multiplier depending on $c$. The interactive view samples a discrete Gaussian free field on a torus for a chosen $c$, visualizes its height landscape, and estimates the growth of squared height differences with distance against the predicted slope.
