Petty projection-volume (conceptual, result 088)

For a convex body (K\subset\mathbb R^n), Petty's inequality says (\frac{|\Pi K|}{|K|^{n-1}}\ge \kappa_{n-1}^n\kappa_n^{2-n}), with equality exactly for ellipsoids when (n\ge4). Compare body types, dimensions, and the simplex counterexample.

Context

Proves Petty's projection-volume conjecture in dimensions \(n\ge4\): ellipsoids uniquely minimize projection-body volume at fixed body volume. Also establishes the full Lutwak--Petty projection inequalities. In contrast, products of simplices exceed Brannen's proposed simplex maximum for normalized projection-body volume by an exponential factor in every sufficiently large dimension; in dimension \(20\), a product of two \(10\)-simplices beats a \(20\)-simplex by \(22355476/22020096>1).

Relevance

Gives the sharp lower bound (|\Pi K|/|K|^{n-1}\ge \kappa_{n-1}^n\kappa_n^{2-n}) with equality exactly for ellipsoids, completing the projection-body inequality family. Shows normalized projection-body volume is not maximized by simplices in high dimensions, via explicit product-of-simplices counterexamples. These affine-invariant comparisons govern convex geometry, projection-body theory, and extremal volume problems.
