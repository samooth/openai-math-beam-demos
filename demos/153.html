Arithmetic classification of Bernoulli convolutions (conceptual, result 153)

An unbiased Bernoulli convolution (\mu_\lambda) is the distribution of (\sum_{n\ge0}\varepsilon_n\lambda^n), (\varepsilon_n=\pm1). Vary (\lambda\in(0,1)): the histogram estimates (\mu_\lambda), while the arithmetic score of (\alpha=1/\lambda) indicates Pisot-like singular cases. The similarity dimension is (d=\min(1,\log 2/\log(1/\lambda))).

Context

Classifies singular and absolutely continuous unbiased Bernoulli convolutions for every (\lambda\in(0,1)) by an infinite, one-sided approximation condition using explicit finite sets of algebraic units. It also proves singularity at reciprocals of every quartic Salem number in ((1,2)), giving examples beyond reciprocal Pisot parameters.

Relevance

Extends Bernoulli-convolution theory beyond the classical Pisot-parameter singularity cases, supplying an arithmetic criterion for absolute continuity and new singular examples (quartic Salem reciprocals). This sharpens the overlap regime for self-similar measures on the line and informs multifractal and spectral questions for Bernoulli convolutions.
