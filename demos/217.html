The low-temperature Sherrington--Kirkpatrick fluctuation law (conceptual, result 217)

For $\beta>1$, the zero-field Gaussian SK log partition function has variance $\sim c_\beta n^{1/3}$ (standard deviation $\sim n^{1/6}$). The demo exactly enumerates small systems to sample the disorder distribution of $\log Z_n$ and checks the $n^{1/3}$ scaling.

Context

For every fixed inverse temperature $\beta>1$, the theorem determines the fluctuation scale and limiting law of the zero-field Gaussian Sherrington--Kirkpatrick log partition function. Its variance is asymptotic to $c_\beta n^{1/3}$ with $c_\beta>0$, confirming the predicted $n^{1/6}$ standard-deviation scale. Exact centering and standardization give full-sequence convergence to a uniquely characterized nondegenerate law.

Relevance

Identifies the low-temperature fluctuation universality class of the SK spin glass: fluctuations are much larger than the $O(1)$ high-temperature scale and occur on $n^{1/3}$ variance / $n^{1/6}$ standard deviation. This underpins the Tracy--Widom-type limiting law and distinguishes the spin-glass phase from replica-symmetric high temperature.
