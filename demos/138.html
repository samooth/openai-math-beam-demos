Subset Sum in (O(2^{0.49n})) time (conceptual, result 138)

Gives a uniform randomized classical algorithm for worst-case Subset Sum in ordinary (O(2^{0.49n})) word-RAM time on polynomial-bit inputs, where (n) counts the integers.

Context

Subset Sum asks whether some submultiset of (n) positive integers sums to a target. Result 138 gives a uniform randomized classical decision algorithm whose worst-case running time is (O(2^{0.49n})) in the word-RAM model on polynomial-bit inputs. The time bound holds on every random execution, and the success probability is at least (2/3) on every input. Inputs may repeat positive integers, and machine words have (O(n+b)) bits for maximum input bit length (b). A companion low-space variant uses (\operatorname{poly}(n)2^{n/2}) time and only (O(2^{n/5})) writable words of (O(n+b)) bits, with one-sided error: it always rejects unsolvable instances and accepts each solvable instance with probability at least (2/3).

Relevance

This improves the classical meet-in-the-middle bound (\Theta(2^{n/2})) time by an exponential factor (2^{0.01n}), and separates time from space: the low-space variant keeps the (2^{n/2})-scale time while reducing writable space from (2^{n/2}) words to (2^{n/5}) words. The interactive view compares brute force, meet-in-the-middle, the new (2^{0.49n}) bound, and the low-space variant as functions of (n), and reports the resulting time and space savings for the chosen parameters.
