Tangent-bundle splittings and universal covers (conceptual, result 052)

A holomorphic splitting (TX=E\oplus F) of the tangent bundle of a compact connected Kähler manifold can induce a compatible product decomposition (\tilde X\simeq Y_1\times Y_2) of the universal cover. Tune the ranks and integrability hypotheses to see when the product structure is forced.

Context

Proves the two-summand form of Beauville's splitting conjecture: for a compact connected Kähler manifold whose tangent bundle splits holomorphically into two positive-rank integrable subbundles, the universal cover is a product of connected simply connected complex manifolds of the prescribed dimensions, and the differential identifies the two tangent factors with the original summands. Both integrability hypotheses are required. On smooth rationally connected projective manifolds, both summands are automatically integrable, establishing Höring's conjecture.

Relevance

Connects a holomorphic decomposition of tangent spaces to a global product structure of the universal cover, with consequences for de Rham/Beauville-type decompositions and for product decompositions of rationally connected manifolds. The automatic integrability case shows that on rationally connected projective manifolds, the existence of a holomorphic tangent splitting already implies the corresponding product decomposition.
