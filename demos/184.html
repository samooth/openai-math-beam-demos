Coloring and independence in graphs with forbidden subgraphs (conceptual, result 184)

Compare the new bounds with trivial baselines: correspondence coloring of (F)-free graphs and independence in (K_r)-free graphs.

Context

Proves the Alon--Krivelevich--Sudakov coloring conjecture in correspondence-coloring form: graphs avoiding any fixed subgraph (F) need (O_F(\Delta/\log\Delta)) colors when their maximum degree (\Delta) is sufficiently large. Also proves the Ajtai--Erdős--Komlós--Szemerédi independence conjecture: for fixed (r\ge4), every (n)-vertex (K_r)-free graph of average degree (d\ge2) has an independent set of size (\Omega_r(n\log d/d)).

Relevance

Supplies optimal (up to constants) coloring and independence bounds for graphs with forbidden subgraphs, improving classical greedy and random arguments and clarifying the role of forbidden structure in extremal graph theory.
