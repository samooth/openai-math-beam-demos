Bounded klt complements for Fano contractions (conceptual, result 066)

For an (\epsilon)-lc Fano contraction (f:X\to Z), tune dimension, discrepancy gap, base dimension, coefficients, and a trial local equation to see the threshold inequalities (t\le a(E;X,B)/\operatorname{ord}_E(f^*g)), the resulting uniform Cartier section, and the bounded klt complement index (N(d,\epsilon)).

Context

Proves the finite-rational-coefficient form of Shokurov's bounded-klt-complement conjecture for (\epsilon)-lc complex Fano-type pairs with nef anti-log-canonical divisor. For every positive integer (d) and positive rational (\epsilon), and every projective surjective morphism (f:X\to Z) of normal integral varieties with connected fibres, (\dim X=d), (K_X) (\mathbb Q)-Cartier, (X) (\epsilon)-lc, and (-K_X) ample over (Z), there is an index (N=N(d,\epsilon)) such that near every closed point (z\in Z) there is an effective (\mathbb Q)-divisor (B) with ((X,B)) klt over a neighbourhood of (z) and (N(K_X+B)) integral Cartier and linearly trivial there. The divisor and neighbourhood may depend on (f) and (z), while (N) depends only on (d) and (\epsilon). Over (\mathbb C), finite rational coefficients are handled, and the stronger real-boundary Cartier-section statement follows via Chen's equivalence.

Relevance

Turns relative positivity of (-K_X) into a klt adjoint divisor with uniformly bounded torsion: instead of merely an lc complement (which may have zero discrepancies), the singularities remain strictly klt. This is the local threshold/uniform-Cartier-section content behind bounded complements, supports moduli and boundedness of Fano-type pairs, and separates the lc case (zero discrepancies allowed) from the klt case (all discrepancies positive). The argument combines normalized-volume minimization, bounded lc complements, Chen's reduction, and pinning obstructions.
