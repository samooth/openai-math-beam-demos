Eisenbud–Green–Harris and lex-plus-powers (conceptual, result 200)

Explores how a regular sequence of powers (x_i^{d}) and an added lexicographic segment determine the Hilbert function of a lex-plus-powers ideal.

Context

The Eisenbud–Green–Harris and lex-plus-powers conjectures are proved over every characteristic-zero field. If a homogeneous ideal contains a regular sequence of degrees at least two, then there is a lex-plus-powers ideal with the same Hilbert function and no smaller graded Betti numbers in every homological and internal degree. The base regular sequence may have arbitrary positive length. The demo below works with a regular sequence of equal powers (x_1^d,\dots,x_c^d) and an added initial lexicographic segment in degree (e), then counts monomials not divisible by any generator to display the resulting quotient Hilbert function.

Relevance

This gives extremal control of Hilbert functions and Betti numbers for ideals containing a regular sequence: the lex-plus-powers representative is as large as possible in Betti numbers among ideals with the given Hilbert function. It supports degree bounds, regularity estimates, and the study of minimal free resolutions in commutative algebra. The interactive view lets you change the number of variables, the length and degree of the regular sequence, and the size of the lexicographic segment, then compare the quotient Hilbert function before and after adding that segment.
