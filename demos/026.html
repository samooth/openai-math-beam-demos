Large prime gaps: positive density

For primes \(p_n\), the gap \(g_n=p_{n+1}-p_n\). Gaps exceeding \(C\log p_n\) are highlighted; the result says a positive proportion of them do, for every fixed \(C>0).

Context

Positive lower density of large prime gaps. For every fixed C > 0, a positive proportion of consecutive prime gaps exceed C log p_n, throughout every sufficiently large initial segment of the primes. The proportion may depend on C. Consequently, the indices where p_n/n increases have positive lower density, answering Erdős and Prachar.

Relevance

Gives an unconditional positive lower density of prime gaps larger than any fixed multiple of log p, and answers Erdős–Prachar on the density of indices where p_n/n increases.
