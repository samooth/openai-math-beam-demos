Logarithmic and L_p Brunn-Minkowski inequalities (conceptual, result 091)

For origin-symmetric convex bodies K,L in R^n, logarithmic and L_p combinations have volume bounded below by the corresponding interpolation of volumes.

Context

Proves the logarithmic Brunn-Minkowski inequality for origin-symmetric convex bodies in every dimension, and the scalar-dilation B-conjecture for all even log-concave Radon measures. For Lebesgue volume it also proves the additive L_p Brunn-Minkowski inequality for full-dimensional origin-symmetric convex bodies throughout 0<p<1. Neither boundary smoothness nor coordinatewise unconditionality is assumed.

Relevance

This resolves the logarithmic Brunn-Minkowski conjecture and the B-conjecture, giving a common interpolation framework between classical Brunn-Minkowski and Minkowski addition. It has consequences for convex geometry, isoperimetric-type inequalities, and the geometry of log-concave measures.
