GOE bulk universality for random regular graphs with weak disorder (conceptual, result 219)

Generates uniform simple random $d$-regular graphs, computes adjacency spectra, and compares bulk nearest-neighbor spacing and density to GOE predictions. Weak diagonal disorder can be added.

Context

For every fixed degree $d\ge3$, the bulk adjacency-eigenvalue point process of a uniform simple random $d$-regular graph converges to the Gaussian orthogonal ensemble law, including for cubic graphs. The same fixed-energy universality persists under sufficiently weak fixed iid uniform diagonal disorder, throughout compact bands strictly inside the clean spectral edges.

Relevance

Connects random regular graphs to GOE local statistics in the bulk, predicting Wigner-Dyson level repulsion rather than Poisson clustering. The interactive view resamples finite graphs, computes spectra, and visualizes how level spacings approach the Wigner surmise while disorder remains inside the permitted weak regime.
