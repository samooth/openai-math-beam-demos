The double-dimer $\mathrm{CLE}_4$ conjecture in the half-plane (conceptual, result 226)

Two independent dimer coverings of the Temperleyan square lattice superpose into a loop ensemble; in the scaling limit these loops converge to nested $\mathrm{CLE}_4$, with every macroscopic loop matched as an unparametrized curve.

Context

Resolves the half-plane Temperleyan form of the double-dimer scaling-limit conjecture: the complete loop ensemble formed by two independent dimer coverings of the Temperleyan square lattice converges to nested $\mathrm{CLE}_4$. Convergence matches every macroscopic loop as an unparametrized curve, upgrading convergence of loop observables to convergence of the loops themselves.

Relevance

Connects discrete dimer models to conformal loop ensembles in the half-plane, upgrading loop-observable convergence to convergence of the loops themselves and supporting conformal invariance of random curve ensembles.
