Quantum geometric Langlands at irrational level (conceptual, result 069)

For a connected simple complex group (G) and its Langlands dual (G^\vee), an irrational shifted level (c\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Q) determines a dual level (-1/(r c)) (with lacing number (r)). The theorem identifies the full derived categories of twisted (D)-modules on (\operatorname{Bun}_G(X)) and (\operatorname{Bun}_{G^\vee}(X)) for every smooth projective curve (X), retaining all global forms and connected components. Explore the group, level, and duality.

Context

Proves the unramified de Rham quantum geometric Langlands equivalence for every connected simple complex algebraic group on every smooth projective connected complex curve, at every shifted level (c\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Q). It identifies the full derived categories of twisted (D)-modules on (\operatorname{Bun}_G(X)) and (\operatorname{Bun}_{G^\vee}(X)) at the dual shifted levels (c) and (-1/(rc)), where (r) is the lacing number. The equivalence uses the given global forms and includes all connected components, including non-real levels.

Relevance

Establishes a uniform, group-independent Langlands duality at generic (irrational) levels: the moduli stacks of principal bundles for (G) and (G^\vee) have equivalent twisted (D)-module categories, so Hecke eigensheaves and automorphic categories match without case-by-case constructions. Retaining global forms and components makes the statement complete for all isogeny types, not just simply connected or adjoint groups, and the explicit dual level (-1/(rc)) pins down the central-charge transformation. This underpins geometric representation theory, the study of opers and conformal blocks, and any application relying on a universal quantum Langlands correspondence.
