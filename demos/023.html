Patterson's first moment for cubic Gauss sums

For primary Eisenstein primes p≡1 (mod 3), normalized cubic Gauss sums g(p) are unit-modulus complex numbers. The first moment Σ g(p) is predicted to have a positive main term ~ (6/5)c_* X^{5/6}/log X. Here we plot a synthetic model of g(p) versus primes and compare its cumulative moment to the predicted curve.

Context

Proves unconditionally the all-primary-prime form of Patterson’s first-moment asymptotic: normalized cubic Gauss sums over primary Eisenstein primes of norm at most X, including both conjugates, have an explicit positive main term of order X^{5/6}/log X. Every fixed nonzero prime-angle Fourier mode has smaller order.

Relevance

Establishes the sharp-cutoff first moment with main term (6/5)c_* X^{5/6}/log X, c_*=(2π)^{2/3}/(3Γ(2/3)), together with angular cancellation for every integer Fourier mode, including all primary Eisenstein primes without a conjectural premise.
