Fontaine–Mazur modularity at the prime 2 (result 010)

Select parameters of a continuous two-dimensional 2-adic Galois representation of (G_{\mathbb Q}). The demo indicates whether the theorems give pro-modular occurrence in completed Hecke algebras and/or classical modularity up to Tate twist.

Context

Proves that every continuous, irreducible, odd two-dimensional 2-adic representation of (G_{\mathbb Q}), unramified outside finitely many primes and de Rham at 2 with distinct Hodge–Tate weights, is modular up to Tate twist. This resolves the odd, regular two-dimensional Fontaine–Mazur conjecture over (\mathbb Q) at 2, including all residual representations (scalar, reducible, absolutely irreducible). Separately, unrestricted pro-modularity at 2 shows every such representation that is odd and absolutely irreducible occurs in a completed Hecke algebra at some odd tame level, with no de Rham hypothesis.

Relevance

Removes the residual restrictions at the prime 2 and completes the odd regular Fontaine–Mazur theorem at every prime. The weight-two case supplies a modularity input used in the undecidability proof for Hilbert's tenth problem over (\mathbb Q). It also connects Galois representations to classical cuspidal eigenforms, allowing Tate twists.
