Termination of projective and Kähler fourfold minimal model programs (conceptual, result 056)

Termination of every existing generalized log canonical flip sequence on globally Weil (\mathbb Q)-factorial compact Kähler fourfolds, and of arbitrary permitted minimal model programs for projective log canonical fourfolds with rational boundary in characteristic zero. Tune the hypotheses and step through an MMP chain to see the well-founded decrease that forces termination.

Context

Proves termination of every existing generalized log canonical flip sequence on globally Weil (\mathbb Q)-factorial compact Kähler fourfolds, with rational boundary, fixed rational analytically nef b-data, and projective small flip diagrams with the prescribed opposite ample signs. Also proves termination of arbitrary permitted minimal model programs for projective log canonical fourfolds with rational boundary in characteristic zero. The theorem concerns existing flip sequences; it does not assert existence of all contractions or flips.

Relevance

Makes fourfold minimal model programs well-founded: flips and mixed birational steps cannot continue indefinitely, so a nef minimal model or a Mori fiber space is reached after finitely many steps. This supports birational classification, Iitaka fibrations, and good minimal models in dimension four, and extends termination beyond the projective setting by using a fixed nef b-divisor and projective small diagrams.
