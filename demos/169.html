Shareshian–Wachs elementary positivity (conceptual, result 169)

For a natural unit interval graph (G) on ([n]), enumerate proper colorings, compute monomial coefficients, convert to the elementary basis, and verify nonnegativity. Also displays the permutation witness (D_G^0) degree distribution.

Context

Resolves the elementary-positivity part of the Shareshian–Wachs conjecture: the chromatic quasisymmetric function of every natural unit interval graph has elementary-basis coefficients in (\mathbb N[q]). The coefficients count explicitly described permutations, giving a combinatorial explanation of positivity. Formalized uniformly in (n) and (G).

Relevance

Specializing (q=1) proves the classical Stanley–Stembridge elementary positivity for incomparability graphs of ((3+1))-free posets. The refined degree data also gives, via Brosnan–Chow/Guay-Paquet, the cohomology of regular semisimple Hessenberg varieties as direct sums of Young permutation modules.
