Numerical semiampleness and generalized minimal models (conceptual, result 036)

For a klt pair ((X,B)) with (K_X+B) pseudo-effective and (M) nef, the adjoint (D=K_X+B+M), when nef, need not be semiample as a line bundle; but it is numerically semiample: there is a semiample (L) with (D\equiv L) (same real Chern class). Tune dimension, numerical dimension, flat part, and pseudo-effectivity to see the status. Generalized lc pairs with pseudo-effective adjoints admit minimal models; otherwise Mori fiber spaces, with nef b-data fixed.

Context

Proves numerical semiampleness for nef adjoints (K_X+B+M) with (K_X+B) pseudo-effective and (M) nef rational, for projective klt rational pairs over algebraically closed characteristic-zero fields and smooth compact Kähler rational klt simple-normal-crossing pairs, using Bott–Chern cohomology in the latter case. Separately, projective generalized log canonical rational pairs over such fields admit minimal models for pseudo-effective adjoints and Mori fiber spaces otherwise, with nef b-data fixed.

Relevance

Replaces direct semiampleness by numerical equivalence, allowing a flat change of line bundle; turns nef adjoints into semiample morphisms up to numerical equivalence, supporting minimal models, Iitaka fibrations, and generalized pairs with fixed nef b-data.
