Counterexamples to the Hadwiger and Colin de Verdière conjectures (conceptual, result 157)

For a graph \(G\), compare the fractional chromatic number \(\chi_f(G)\), the Hadwiger number \(h(G)\), and the Colin de Verdière number \(\mu(G)\). Result 157 gives graphs with independence number at most two for which \(\chi_f(G)>h(G)\) and \(\chi_f(G)>\mu(G)+1), while list coloring remains linearly bounded by (h(G)).

Context

Disproves Hadwiger's conjecture even for fractional coloring: arbitrarily large finite simple graphs with independence number at most two satisfy \(\chi_f(G)>h(G)), where (h(G)) is the largest clique-minor order. Also disproves the fractional Colin de Verdière chromatic bound (\chi_f(G)\le \mu(G)+1). In the positive direction, every finite nonempty graph satisfies (\chi_{\mathrm{list}}(G)\le Ch(G)) for a universal constant (C).

Relevance

Separates the classical Hadwiger and Colin de Verdière chromatic conjectures from their fractional relaxations and shows that clique-minor size still controls list coloring up to a constant. This clarifies which graph invariants govern coloring variants and redirects extremal graph theory toward list-coloring and minor-based bounds.
