Weak inhomogeneous Duffin–Schaeffer

For shift γ and ψ, test whether ||q x − γ|| < ψ(q) for some q ≤ Q, for sampled x. Criterion: ∑ φ(q)ψ(q)/q diverges.

Context

Proves that for every real shift γ and finite-valued ψ:ℕ→[0,∞), divergence of ∑_q φ(q)ψ(q)/q implies ||q x − γ|| < ψ(q) for infinitely many q, for almost every x. Here φ is Euler's totient and the norm is distance to the nearest integer. Numerators are unrestricted; no monotonicity or Diophantine condition on γ is needed.

Relevance

Removes the monotonicity and Diophantine (inhomogeneous) hypotheses from the Duffin–Schaeffer conjecture, giving a fully general metric Diophantine approximation theorem for arbitrary shifts.
