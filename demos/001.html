Milne's rationality conjecture

Specialized Hodge classes on an abelian variety pair rationally with complementary divisor products. The grid shows the pairing matrix (minors of a polarization matrix) for a small example; entries are integers, hence rational.

Context

Proves Milne's rationality conjecture for abelian varieties over \bar Q with good reduction: specialized Hodge classes pair rationally with complementary divisor products, independently of cohomology theory. Together with result 032, every specialized Hodge class is represented by a single rational algebraic cycle simultaneously in all prime-to-p and crystalline realizations, for every residue characteristic p.

Relevance

Shows Hodge classes are rational algebraic cycles in all realizations, a key step toward the Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties and toward arithmetic Hodge theory.
