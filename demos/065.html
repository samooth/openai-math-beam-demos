Virasoro constraints for complete intersections and projective bundles (conceptual, result 065)

The Virasoro constraints are an infinite system of differential equations on Gromov-Witten potentials. Tune the operator index, genus and degree bounds, and insertion count to see how they recursively determine descendant invariants for complete intersections and projective-bundle towers.

Context

Proves the full ordinary unreduced descendant Virasoro conjecture for smooth complete intersections in complex projective space, in every genus and curve class, with arbitrary cohomology insertions. The constraints also pass from any smooth projective complex base satisfying them to the projectivization of every algebraic vector bundle of rank at least two, and hence to projective-bundle towers. Together this gives a uniform family of differential constraints on the Gromov-Witten theory of these spaces.

Relevance

Turns enumerative geometry into a recursive computation: once the base invariants are known, the Virasoro operators determine higher-genus and higher-degree descendant invariants without new moduli integrals. This underpins mirror symmetry computations, quantum Lefschetz and projectivization formulas, and gives structural control over Gromov-Witten potentials for complete intersections and iterated projective bundles.
