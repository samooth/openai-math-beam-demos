The ordinary-double-point volume gap (result 037)

Singular klt germ of dimension n: normalized volume at most 2(n-1)^n, with equality only for an analytic ordinary double point. Smooth points attain n^n. Plot shows both bounds versus dimension.

Context

Proves the ordinary-double-point volume-gap conjecture for boundary-zero complex algebraic klt germs in every dimension n ≥ 2: a singular n-dimensional germ has normalized volume at most 2(n-1)^n, with equality precisely at an analytic ordinary double point.

Relevance

Distinguishes the simplest singular ODP by a sharp numerical invariant; combined with the local-to-global volume inequality, gives global bounds for K-semistable Fano varieties and classifies equality cases such as smooth quadrics.
