Koebe's circle-domain conjecture and circle-domain rigidity (conceptual, result 071)

Every domain in the Riemann sphere is conformally equivalent to a circle domain whose complementary components are round disks or points. If the boundary is conformally removable, such a circle domain is rigid: every conformal equivalence to another circle domain is Möbius. Tune the components and the map below.

Context

Resolves the existence part of Koebe's circle-domain conjecture: every domain in the Riemann sphere is conformally equivalent to a domain whose complementary components are round disks or points. It also proves that circle domains with conformally removable boundary are rigid, meaning every conformal equivalence to another circle domain is Möbius, establishing this direction of the He–Schramm conjecture.

Relevance

Uniformizes arbitrary planar domains by round disks, reducing conformal classification to circle domains; rigidity says that once boundary components are removable, the conformal structure is determined by Möbius maps, so no nontrivial moduli remain. This supports circle packing, discrete conformal geometry, and the study of conformal removability.
