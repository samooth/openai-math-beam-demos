Two-point Chowla / corrected Elliott

Liouville correlation sums (S_h(X)=\sum_{n\le X}\lambda(n)\lambda(n+h)). Chowla predicts (S_h(X)=o(X)); here (S_h(X)/X\to 0) for fixed shifts (h\neq 0).

Context

Proves the ordinary two-point Chowla conjecture, with a bound \(O(X/(\log X)^c)\) for Liouville correlation sums along fixed nonproportional affine forms, where \(c>0) is absolute. More generally, proves the binary corrected Elliott conjecture for complex multiplicative functions bounded by one when one factor is uniformly nonpretentious against each fixed Dirichlet character times (n^{it}) for (|t|\le X).

Relevance

Gives unconditional cancellation in ordinary (non-logarithmic) two-point averages of multiplicative functions, a key input toward the Chowla and Sarnak conjectures and to distribution of multiplicative functions in short intervals.
