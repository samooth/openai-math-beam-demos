Sampling and counting contingency tables with arbitrary margins (conceptual, result 115)

Nonnegative integer matrices with prescribed row and column sums: exact uniform sampling in expected polynomial bit time, almost-uniform sampling in worst-case polynomial bit time, and an FPRAS for counts with arbitrary cell bounds.

Context

For nonnegative integer matrices with prescribed row and column sums, the result gives exact uniform sampling in expected polynomial bit time and almost-uniform sampling in worst-case polynomial bit time. The dimensions and binary-encoded margins are unrestricted. It also gives a fully polynomial randomized approximation scheme for counting such tables with arbitrary individual cell bounds, including structural zeros, with polynomial cost on every execution.

Relevance

Uniform contingency tables with fixed margins are the combinatorial core of exact conditional inference, randomization tests, and Markov-chain Monte Carlo diagnostics. The new sampler removes dimension and margin restrictions, while the counting FPRAS handles cell bounds and structural zeros, making both exact sampling and approximate counting available in polynomial bit time.
