The Mézard--Parisi formula for diluted spin glasses (conceptual, result 221)

Poisson-diluted even-arity Ising models: exact free energy on small random factor graphs compared with the Bethe/cavity estimate, illustrating hierarchical trial laws.

Context

For Poisson-diluted even-arity Ising models satisfying the Panchenko--Talagrand factorization and positivity assumptions, with only first-moment integrability, the limiting free energy equals the infimum over finite-depth hierarchical trial laws. This is the Mézard--Parisi hierarchical cavity formula. It covers the Viana--Bray model, symmetric diluted even-spin models, and weighted soft even-$K$ satisfiability. The cavity fields are organized by an ultrametric hierarchy; each additional level lowers (or leaves) the variational free energy, and the limit gives the exact thermodynamic free energy.

Relevance

Provides a rigorous variational formula for diluted spin glasses where interactions are sparse and locally tree-like. The hierarchical form explains replica-symmetry breaking in diluted systems and underpins message-passing algorithms, inference, and constraint-satisfaction thresholds. The interactive view builds a small random factor graph, computes the exact partition function, and compares it with the Bethe cavity estimate as disorder, temperature, and arity vary.
