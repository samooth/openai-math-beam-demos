Relevance

Settles the diagonal case of the restriction problem for positively curved surfaces in \(\mathbb R^3\), fixing the sharp threshold \(p>3\). It advances beyond earlier bounded-data and partial diagonal estimates, and yields a free Schrödinger local smoothing estimate with Sobolev threshold \(s>2-6/p), plus scaling (N^{-3/p}) for associated oscillatory integrals. These feed restriction theory, dispersive estimates, and local smoothing for the Schrödinger equation.
