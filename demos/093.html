Dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev inequality for subgaussian log-concave measures (conceptual, result 093)

For a centered log-concave probability measure on (\mathbb R^n) with linear subgaussian parameter (a), the entropy of any smooth test satisfies (\operatorname{Ent}_\mu(f^2)\le C a^2\int |Df|^2d\mu) with one universal (C). Explore how prior bounds grew with dimension and how subgaussian tails look.

Context

Proves a dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev inequality for centered log-concave densities with uniformly subgaussian linear marginals, with constant bounded by a universal multiple of the squared linear subgaussian parameter. Every centered log-concave probability measure with a Lebesgue density on (\mathbb R^n) and linear subgaussian parameter (a) satisfies (\operatorname{Ent}_\mu(f^2)\le C a^2\int |Df|^2d\mu) for compactly supported smooth (f), with one universal constant (C). This resolves positively the dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev conjecture for subgaussian log-concave measures.

Relevance

A dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev inequality gives Gaussian concentration and quadratic transport-entropy bounds independent of dimension for log-concave measures whose one-dimensional projections have Gaussian tails. It improves earlier criteria whose constants grew with (n), and applies to uniform measures on convex bodies without curvature assumptions.
