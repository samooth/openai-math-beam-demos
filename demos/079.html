Relevance

Gives the sharp local smoothing threshold (s(p)=\max\{0,1-3/p\}) for the three-dimensional wave propagator in (L^p). It controls how much Sobolev regularity is forced (or gained) in localized space-time regions, including the critical (L^3) case with arbitrarily small loss. This refines dispersive and microlocal estimates for the wave equation and supports harmonic-analysis and PDE arguments that require precise (L^p) control of wave solutions.
