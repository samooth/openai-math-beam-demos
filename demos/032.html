Rational Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties (conceptual, result 032)

A CM abelian variety has cohomology split into one-dimensional embedding lines. A tensor is a rational Hodge class iff, in every scalar conjugate, the number of holomorphic factors equals the number of antiholomorphic factors (Pohlmann balance). Toggle the sign matrix (rows = factors, columns = conjugate pairs) and check balance.

Context

Proves the rational Hodge conjecture for every complex CM abelian variety, in every dimension and codimension: the Betti cycle-class map from Chow groups to rational Hodge classes is surjective. Through Milne's theorems, this also gives the Tate conjecture for all abelian varieties over finite fields and the Hodge standard conjecture for abelian varieties in every characteristic.

Relevance

Identifies rational Hodge classes with rational linear combinations of algebraic cycle classes on CM abelian varieties; yields the generalized Hodge conjecture for CM varieties and, via Milne, Tate and Hodge-standard consequences in arbitrary characteristic.
