Nagata's conjecture / Seshadri constants (result 039)

For a nonzero effective plane curve of degree (d) through (r\ge 10) very general points with multiplicities (m_i), Nagata requires (\sum_i m_i < d\sqrt r\). Edit the multiplicities to test the inequality; the Seshadri panel gives the maximal constant \((L^n/r)^{1/n}\).

Context

Proves Nagata's strict inequality (\sum_i m_i < d\sqrt r\) for every nonzero effective plane curve of degree \(d\) through \(r\ge 10\) very general complex points, with arbitrary multiplicities \(m_i\). It also proves maximal multipoint Seshadri constants \((L^n/r)^{1/n}\) for every smooth polarized projective variety of dimension \(n\ge 2\) and all sufficiently large \(r\): at very general points over \(\mathbb C\), and at the geometric generic tuple over any algebraically closed field of positive characteristic.

Relevance

Gives the sharp Nagata bound for plane curves with unequal multiplicities and reducible components, and shows multipoint Seshadri constants attain the volume upper bound ((L^n/r)^{1/n}) for large (r), a central positivity statement in higher-dimensional geometry.
