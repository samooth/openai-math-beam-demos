Relevance

This resolves the simultaneous additive--multiplicative Ramsey problem: unlike the separate sums or products theorems, a single color must survive both operations at once. It also shows that arbitrary finite colorings cannot avoid large monochromatic mixed arithmetic configurations, and the separation clause eliminates collisions between expressions, yielding (2(2^m-1)-m) distinct integers in one color. The result connects Ramsey theory, nilsequence prediction, and alignment of additive and multiplicative scales.
