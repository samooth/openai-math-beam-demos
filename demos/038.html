Fujita's freeness conjecture (result 038)

For a smooth projective complex variety (X) of dimension (n) and an ample line bundle (L), the adjoint bundle (K_X+mL) is globally generated for every integer (m\ge n+1). Move (n) and (m) to test the sharp threshold.

Context

Proves Fujita's freeness conjecture at its sharp bound in every dimension: for a smooth projective complex variety (X) of dimension (n) and an ample line bundle (L), the adjoint (K_X+mL) is globally generated for every integer (m\ge n+1).

Relevance

Gives effective global generation of adjoint bundles at the optimal linear bound; sharpness means (n+1) cannot be lowered in general. Foundational for positivity and adjunction theory, basepoint-free pencils, and classification in higher dimensions.
