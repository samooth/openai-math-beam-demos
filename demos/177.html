Bounded-degree coboundary expanders in every dimension (conceptual, result 177)

Samples a random $2$-dimensional simplicial complex (clique complex of a random graph) and computes its $\mathbb F_2$ coboundary expansion constants in degrees $0$ and $1$ by direct linear algebra over $\mathbb F_2$.

Context

Result 177 constructs arbitrarily large finite $d$-dimensional simplicial complexes, for every $d\ge3$, with uniformly bounded vertex degrees and uniform $\mathbb F_2$ coboundary expansion in every degree below $d$. Together with the known graph ($d=1$) and two-dimensional cases, this establishes the existence of such expanders in every positive dimension. The demo samples a small random $2$-complex and measures the corresponding constants directly.

Relevance

Coboundary expanders are high-dimensional analogues of expander graphs; they provide sparse objects with strong isoperimetric properties in every dimension. Their existence with bounded degree underpins constructions in high-dimensional topology, locally testable codes, quantum CSS codes, and property testing that were previously unavailable.
