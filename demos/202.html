The blockwise Alperin weight conjecture (conceptual, result 202)

For a finite group $G$ and prime $p$, each $p$-block $B$ has irreducible Brauer characters and $B$-weights. The conjecture says their counts coincide: $k(B)=w(B)$.

Context

Proves the numerical blockwise Alperin weight conjecture for every finite group and every prime $p$: for each $p$-block $B$, the number of irreducible Brauer characters in $B$ equals the number of conjugacy classes of $B$-weights.

Relevance

Gives a uniform counting formula for blocks in modular representation theory and supports blockwise invariants, defect groups, and the broader Alperin weight program.
