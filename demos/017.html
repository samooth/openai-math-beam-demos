π: irrationality exponent = 2

Best rational approximations p/q to π (continued-fraction convergents). The plot shows log₁₀(error) vs log₁₀(q); a slope of −2 means error ~ q⁻².

Context

Proves that for every ε>0 and large q, |π−p/q| ≥ q^(−2−ε). Convergence of the Flint–Hills series follows.

Relevance

Settles the exact irrationality exponent of π, with consequences for the Flint–Hills series.
