Massive continuum limits and exact mass asymptotics for planar O(n) models (conceptual, result 215)

Conceptual interactive view of exponential spin-correlation decay, the exact O(4) transfer-gap asymptotic, and the positive mass gap of the O(3) continuum limit.

Context

The two-dimensional nearest-neighbor $O(n)$ model places spins on $S^{n-1}$ with Boltzmann weight $\exp(\sum b_{xy}\sigma_x\cdot\sigma_y)$. For $n\ge 3$ and every finite $\beta>0$, correlations decay exponentially: $0\le \langle \sigma_x\cdot \sigma_y\rangle \le A e^{-m|x-y|_2}$. The $O(3)$ model has a canonical massive continuum limit: a non-Gaussian local relativistic theory with a unique vacuum and a positive mass gap. For the square-lattice $O(4)$ model, the full transfer gap has exact leading asymptotic $m_{\mathrm{lat}}(\beta)\sim 32e^{\pi/4-1/2}\sqrt{\beta}e^{-\pi\beta}$.

Relevance

Establishes exponential decay at all positive temperatures for $n\ge3$, ruling out long-range order in the planar continuous-symmetry setting. The $O(3)$ mass gap gives a massive relativistic continuum description, while the $O(4)$ asymptotic gives a sharp low-temperature spectral scale. The demo is conceptual: it visualizes exponential envelopes, the exact $O(4)$ mass curve, and a lattice heatmap; constants for the $O(n)$ decay are illustrative.
