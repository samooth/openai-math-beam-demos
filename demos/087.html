Context

The symmetric Mahler conjecture predicts (|K||K^\circ|\ge 4^n/n!) for every origin-symmetric convex body (K\subset\mathbb R^n). It is proved for every (n\ge1), and equality is characterized exactly by invertible linear images of Hanner bodies (built from intervals by Cartesian products and convex-hull joins). The general Mahler conjecture gives the sharp lower bound ((n+1)^{n+1}/(n!)^2) for the minimized volume product of any compact convex body with nonempty interior; equality holds exactly for simplices. Relevance

These results settle sharp universal lower bounds for volume products under polar duality, completely characterize the equality cases (Hanner bodies and simplices), and determine the maximal symplectic ball that fits in a symmetric polar product. They connect affine convex geometry, functional inequalities, and symplectic embedding theory, and provide exact thresholds separating extremal bodies from all others in every dimension.
