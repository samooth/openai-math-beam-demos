The $\mu$-constant problem for surface singularities (conceptual, result 064)

A holomorphic one-parameter family of isolated hypersurface singularities in $\mathbb C^3$ with constant Milnor number $\mu$ is topologically right-trivial: after shrinking, ambient homeomorphisms varying continuously in $t$ fix the origin section and preserve the defining functions. Vary the family, the parameter, and the Milnor radius to see when the $\mu$-constant hypothesis forces a trivialization.

Context

Proves topological right-triviality for every holomorphic one-parameter family of isolated hypersurface singularities in $\mathbb C^3$ with constant Milnor number, resolving the surface case of the $\mu$-constant problem. After shrinking the parameter disk and representatives, ambient homeomorphisms vary jointly continuously, fix the origin section, and preserve the defining functions. The proof compares Milnor fibers, uses resolution and Wahl's logarithmic invariant, and handles the log-canonical and double-point cases separately.

Relevance

Closes the one dimension where the classical Lê--Ramanujam cobordism argument fails to give a product structure, so constancy of the Milnor number alone suffices for topological triviality of surface singularities. It separates topological triviality from Whitney regularity, distinguishes the $\mu$-constant hypothesis from full Teissier sequences, and feeds into equisingularity, deformation theory, and classification of normal Gorenstein surface singularities.
