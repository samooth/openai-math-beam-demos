Tensor saturation for even spin groups (conceptual, result 204)

For $\operatorname{Spin}(2n)$, $n\ge 2$: if three dominant integral weights with sum in the root lattice admit an invariant at some common dilation $k\ge1$, then they already admit one at $k=1$. This is saturation factor one for type $D$.

Context

Proves saturation factor one for $\operatorname{Spin}(2n)$, $n\ge2$: for three dominant integral weights whose sum lies in the root lattice, an invariant at any common positive integral dilation already gives an invariant at the original weights. This resolves the type-$D$ part of the simply-laced saturation conjecture.

Relevance

Completes the simply-laced saturation picture for even spin groups: no dilation factor is needed in type $D$, so tensor-product invariants, Littlewood–Richardson-type multiplicities, and hive/inequality descriptions can be read at the original weights. This supports representation-theoretic computations, quantum cohomology, and geometric invariant theory for $\operatorname{Spin}(2n)$.
