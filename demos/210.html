Foulkes' conjecture for sixth powers and quadratic stabilization (conceptual, result 210)

Compares the dimensions of $\operatorname{Sym}^a(\operatorname{Sym}^b V)$ and $\operatorname{Sym}^b(\operatorname{Sym}^a V)$, the Foulkes embedding condition $a\le b$, and the quadratic stabilization bound $b\ge a(a-1)$.

Context

Foulkes' conjecture asks, for $a\le b$, for a $\mathrm{GL}(V)$-equivariant injection $\operatorname{Sym}^a(\operatorname{Sym}^b V)\hookrightarrow \operatorname{Sym}^b(\operatorname{Sym}^a V)$. Result 210 proves the sixth case: $\operatorname{Sym}^6(\operatorname{Sym}^b V)$ embeds in $\operatorname{Sym}^b(\operatorname{Sym}^6 V)$ for every $b\ge 6$. More generally, the canonical multiplication (Foulkes--Howe) map $\operatorname{Sym}^b(\operatorname{Sym}^a V)\to \operatorname{Sym}^a(\operatorname{Sym}^b V)$ is surjective whenever $a\ge 2$ and $b\ge a(a-1)$, giving a dimension-independent quadratic stabilization bound. The demo computes the module dimensions for a chosen vector-space dimension $n=\dim V$ and powers $a,b$, illustrating the necessary dimension inequality and the two thresholds.

Relevance

This is a central problem in representation theory of $\mathrm{GL}(V)$ and invariant theory: it controls how symmetric powers of symmetric powers decompose and gives stabilization bounds for canonical maps. The quadratic bound $b\ge a(a-1)$ is independent of $\dim V$, so it applies uniformly to all finite-dimensional complex $V$. The interactive view lets you vary $\dim V$, $a$, and $b$, check the Foulkes condition $a\le b$, the stabilization condition $b\ge a(a-1)$, and compare the two module dimensions (a necessary condition for any injection or surjection).
