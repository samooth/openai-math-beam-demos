The ℓ¹-Bass and complex Bass trace conjectures (conceptual, result 207)

For a discrete group $G$, Hattori--Stallings traces of idempotents over $\ell^1(G)$ are supported on finitely many finite-order conjugacy classes; over $\mathbb C[G]$, traces vanish on infinite-order classes. For torsion-free $G$, idempotents are $0$ or $1$.

Context

Proves the $\ell^1$-Bass conjecture for every discrete group: Hattori--Stallings traces of idempotent matrices over $\ell^1(G)$ are supported on finitely many conjugacy classes, all of finite order. The algebraic companion proves the complex Bass trace conjecture for every group: the trace of each virtual class in $K_0(\mathbb C[G])$ vanishes on conjugacy classes of infinite-order elements, with no finiteness or geometric hypothesis on $G$. For torsion-free $G$, it identifies the trace on $K_0(\mathbb C[G])$ with the integral augmentation rank and proves Kaplansky's idempotent conjecture in characteristic zero: for every commutative unital domain $R$ of characteristic zero, an idempotent in $R[G]$ is $0$ or $1$.

Relevance

Separates finite-order (torsion) data from infinite-order behavior in group-ring traces: finite support in the Banach $\ell^1$ setting and vanishing in the complex algebraic setting. For torsion-free groups it makes the augmentation rank a complete trace invariant and reduces idempotents to trivial ones, with consequences for $K$-theory, operator algebras, and the Kaplansky idempotent program.
