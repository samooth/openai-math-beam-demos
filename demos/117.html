Uniform sparsest cut: hardness and semidefinite gaps (conceptual, result 117)

Approximating Uniform Sparsest Cut within any fixed constant factor is NP-hard, even with nonnegative rational capacities and unit demands. The Goemans--Linial semidefinite relaxation also has integrality gaps of order at least $\sqrt{\log n}/(\log\log n)^3$, approaching the square-root-logarithmic upper bound.

Context

Proves that approximating Uniform Sparsest Cut within any fixed constant factor is NP-hard, even with nonnegative rational capacities and unit demands. The Goemans--Linial semidefinite relaxation also has integrality gaps of order at least $\sqrt{\log n}/(\log\log n)^3$, approaching the square-root-logarithmic upper bound.

Relevance

This rules out constant-factor polynomial-time approximations in the standard weighted model and shows the Goemans--Linial relaxation cannot be rounded to a constant factor in worst case. The near-square-root-logarithmic gap pins the relaxation's limitation close to the known upper bound, guiding the search for stronger relaxations or sharper hardness reductions.
