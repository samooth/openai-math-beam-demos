Context

Proves Brennan's conjecture: for every conformal bijection (\phi) from a simply connected plane domain with nontrivial spherical boundary onto the disk, (|\phi'|^s) is area-integrable for (4/3

Relevance

Resolves a central problem in geometric function theory: the exact integrability range for conformal maps and the value of the integral-means spectrum at (-2). The strict inverse-first-power bound shows Kraetzer's quadratic prediction (B_b(p)=p^2/4) fails for bounded univalent functions, refining the extremal theory of univalent functions and the boundary behavior of conformal maps. It underpins studies of harmonic measure, multifractal spectra, and conformal invariance, and provides sharp constants and divergence examples that calibrate extremal constructions.
