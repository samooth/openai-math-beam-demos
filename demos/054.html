Counterexamples to Kuznetsov's rationality conjecture (conceptual, result 054)

For a very general smooth complex cubic fourfold (X) in a Hassett divisor (C_d), having an untwisted geometric K3 category and an integral Hodge-theoretic K3 association need not imply rationality. Tune the discriminant (d) and the "sufficiently large" threshold to see when the associated-K3 criterion fails.

Context

For every sufficiently large admissible Hassett discriminant, a very general smooth complex cubic fourfold is irrational despite having both an untwisted geometric K3 category and an integral Hodge-theoretic K3 association. This disproves Kuznetsov's rationality conjecture and the sufficiency of the associated-K3 criterion for rationality; the discriminant threshold is ineffective.

Relevance

Shows that possessing an associated K3 surface or category does not force rationality: rationality of cubic fourfolds is not determined by Hodge-theoretic or categorical data alone. It rules out the associated-K3 criterion as a sufficient test and motivates finer invariants (motivic, Chow-theoretic, or lattice-theoretic) for rationality; the ineffective threshold means no finite discriminant bound can rescue the conjecture.
